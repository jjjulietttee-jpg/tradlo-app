Tradlo

trading journal · calendar of P&L · edge by setup

Tradlo is a diary for people who trade stocks, options, futures, forex or crypto and want to see which habits make money and which leak it. Log a trade by hand or bring in a broker CSV, tag the setup and the mistake, then read your edge in R-multiples.
