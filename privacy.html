Privacy

filed 2026-10-06 · follows the life of one trade

01 · entry

What you type in

Symbol, market, side, size, entry, exit, stop, fees, open and close times, setup tag, mistake tags, emotion, a free-text note and your own P&L figure, plus the daily plan and recap in the diary. All of it goes into a database inside the app on your iPhone.

02 · attachments

Chart pictures

A photo you attach to a trade is chosen through the iOS photo picker, so Tradlo receives just that image. It is saved in the app’s private storage on the phone and tied to the trade.

03 · import

Broker files

When you import, iOS opens the Files browser and Tradlo reads the one CSV you pick, on the device, to build trade rows. The file is not uploaded anywhere by us. Samples for IBKR, Robinhood, Schwab, Tradovate and Webull layouts ship inside the app.

04 · analysis

Numbers are computed locally

Calendar, edge, R-multiples and the weekly review are calculated on the phone from your records. No server crunches them, and no AI service is called.

05 · outbound

What does leave the device

to Apple: purchase and renewal
to Adapty: anonymous profile ID, subscription state, paywall views, onboarding step counters
to anyone else: nothing

Adapty is the subscription-management provider. It does not receive your trades, tags, notes, chart images or imported files. Apple keeps the payment method, which stays out of our reach.

06 · export

CSV you send yourself

Export all trades (CSV) in Preferences writes a file on the phone and offers it through the share sheet. Where it lands is your choice.

07 · mail

Write to us

Choosing it opens your mail composer with a draft carrying the app build, the iOS release and your Adapty profile ID. Edit or delete that footer before sending.

08 · alerts

No notifications

The app does not ask to send alerts of any kind.

09 · tracking

None

No advertising identifier is used and no data is traded or linked with outside sets. The app’s privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

10 · exit

Removing it all

In Preferences, under Data, press Delete all data and confirm with Delete: trades, diary entries and stored charts disappear from the phone. One trade can be removed through Delete trade. Deleting the app itself also clears everything. We keep no server copy, so there is nothing for us to wipe on request. The Adapty profile holds only the anonymous ID and subscription state; write to us and we will ask Adapty to remove it.

11 · minors

Age

Tradlo is aimed at adults; we do not knowingly serve children under 13.
