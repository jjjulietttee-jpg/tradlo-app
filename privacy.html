Privacy Policy

Tradlo: Trading Journal & P&L · Last updated September 25, 2026

Tradlo is a trading journal. It is built so that your trades stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your journal, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Trades you log or import: tickers, prices, quantities, P&L, setups, mistakes, emotions and notes.
  • Chart screenshots you attach. They are copied into Tradlo's own storage; Tradlo gets only the pictures you pick, not access to your photo library.
  • Your daily diary and weekly notes, settings and tags.

Broker files

When you import a CSV exported from your broker, the file is read on your iPhone and only the trades are kept. Tradlo never connects to your broker, never asks for broker logins and never uploads the file.

No market data

Tradlo does not download quotes and makes no network requests for your journal.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tradlo receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases; it never receives your trades.

Analytics and tracking

Tradlo does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every trade, chart and diary entry. Deleting the app removes everything too. You can export all trades as CSV from Settings at any time.

Children

Tradlo is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
