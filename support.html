Common questions

Which broker files work? Robinhood account activity, Webull orders, Interactive Brokers Flex trades or activity statement, Schwab history, Tradovate performance, and any CSV with Date, Symbol, Side, Quantity and Price columns.

An imported P&L differs from my broker. Check fees and the date range of the export. Send us the column names (not your data) and we will look.

How is R calculated? From the stop you log: risk = |entry − stop| × quantity × multiplier. Without a stop, from the default risk per trade in Settings.

Restore purchases. Settings → Tradlo Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription. Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Tradlo.
